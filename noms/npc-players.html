THE HOUSE RULES

1 · STICK AN IDEA — type it on the black note up top, sign it or stay anonymous, then hit ✂ STICK IT UP.

2 · REPLY — hit the ⟶ reply arrow on any note and it becomes a black tape strip pinned underneath that note.

3 · BE KIND — the one and only rule. No hate, no spam, no stealing people's ideas.

4 · FIX IT — you can ✎ fix your own note: the old text gets a line through it and the corrected text takes its place, so the wall keeps its history.

5 · PEEL IT — ✕ peel any of your own notes or replies when they've had their moment. They come off the wall for everyone. Moderators peel anything that breaks rule 3.
